Find The Match Game

Welcome to Match the Tile!
Test your memory and speed in this fun tile-matching game.

How to Play:

Choose Your Difficulty:
Easy: 12 images
Medium: 16 images (Coming soon)
Select Number of Rounds

Flipping Tiles:
Click a tile to flip it over.
Click another tile to find its match.
If they match, they stay revealed.
If not, the tiles flip back over

Win the Round:
Match all pairs!
Progress through rounds to increase your score. The more rounds you choose the higher the score!
